Worker page integration

In app/settings/workers/[id]/page.tsx:

1. Import:
import EmploymentProfileForm from "./employment/EmploymentProfileForm";

2. Ensure the worker query includes:
employmentProfile: true,

3. Add this section wherever worker administration cards are rendered:

<section>
  <h2>Employment & Schedule Profile</h2>
  <p>
    Used by Scheduling Intelligence for weekly paid-hour constraints.
  </p>

  <EmploymentProfileForm
    collectorId={worker.id}
    profile={worker.employmentProfile}
  />
</section>

Do not change role eligibility logic.
